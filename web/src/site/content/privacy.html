This Privacy Policy describes how CarefulTO collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you use our website or delivery services. We encourage you to read it carefully. By submitting a booking with CarefulTO, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

Privacy is not an afterthought at CarefulTO — it is a core part of what we do. We handle legal contracts, medical records, confidential business documents, and personal correspondence every day. Our clients trust us with items that are not only physically fragile but often deeply sensitive. That trust extends to the personal information they share with us when booking.

CarefulTO is an owner-operated courier service based in Ontario, Canada. We do not operate a call centre, a warehouse, or a sales team. The person you book with is the person who handles your parcel — and your information. That means your data is seen by as few people as possible, used for exactly the purpose you provided it, and nothing else.

We collect only the information necessary to complete your delivery. We do not build advertising profiles, sell data, or share your details with anyone who does not need them to fulfil your booking. This policy tells you exactly what we collect, why, who sees it, and how we protect it.

This policy applies to personal information collected through our website at carefulto.ca, our booking form, our email correspondence, and any other interaction you have with CarefulTO.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to book, fulfil, and confirm your delivery. This includes:

Information You Provide Directly

  • Full name — to identify the booking and communicate with you
  • Email address — to send booking confirmation, delivery updates, and photo confirmation
  • Phone number — to contact you before pickup or during delivery if needed
  • Pickup address — where we collect the parcel
  • Delivery address — where the parcel is going
  • Parcel type and any special handling notes — to handle your item appropriately
  • Preferred delivery date — to schedule your booking
  • Delivery zone selection — to determine applicable pricing

Payment Information

Payment is processed entirely by Stripe, our third-party payment processor. CarefulTO does not collect, see, store, or have access to your credit card number, CVV, or banking details at any point. When you click "Proceed to Secure Payment," you are redirected to Stripe's hosted checkout. Any payment data you enter is handled exclusively by Stripe under their own privacy and security standards.

Delivery Photos

Upon completion of every delivery, a photograph of the delivered parcel at its destination is taken as proof of delivery. This photo is shared with you — the booking client — by email, and retained briefly by CarefulTO for dispute resolution purposes before deletion.

Information Collected Automatically

Our website is hosted on Netlify. Standard web server logs (such as IP addresses and page request times) may be collected automatically by Netlify as part of normal website operation. CarefulTO does not access or use these logs for tracking or marketing purposes. We do not use cookies for advertising or analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

The personal information you provide is used exclusively to deliver the service you have requested. Specifically:

  • To confirm your booking — we send a written confirmation to your email address once your booking is reviewed and accepted
  • To coordinate pickup — we may contact you by phone or email to confirm the pickup time or address details
  • To complete the delivery — your pickup and delivery addresses are used to navigate to and from the correct locations
  • To send delivery confirmation — we email you the proof-of-delivery photo upon successful delivery
  • To process your payment — your booking details are passed to Stripe to generate a secure, itemized checkout session
  • To handle cancellations or disputes — booking records and delivery photos may be referenced if a cancellation, claim, or dispute arises
  • To maintain business records — completed booking records are retained for a reasonable operational period for accounting and legal compliance purposes

We do not use your information for marketing. We will not add you to a mailing list, send you promotional emails, or contact you for any purpose unrelated to your delivery. The only communications you will receive from CarefulTO are transactional — directly related to a booking you have made.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

CarefulTO does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party for commercial, marketing, or advertising purposes — ever.

Your information may be shared with the following service providers, solely to the extent necessary to fulfil your delivery:

Stripe
Payment Processing Your name, email address, and booking details are passed to Stripe to create a secure checkout session. Stripe processes your payment and issues your receipt. Stripe is a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor — the highest level of security certification available in the payments industry. Stripe's privacy policy governs how they handle your payment data.
Netlify
Website & Form Hosting Our website and booking form are hosted on Netlify, a cloud platform based in the United States. Form submissions may pass through Netlify's infrastructure. Netlify does not use your form data for any purpose other than transmitting it to us. Netlify's privacy policy governs how they handle infrastructure-level data.

No other third parties receive your personal information. All deliveries are performed personally by CarefulTO — we do not subcontract to other couriers or share your pickup or delivery addresses with any external logistics provider.

CarefulTO may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or if we have a good-faith belief that such disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of CarefulTO, our clients, or the public.

5. Method of Disclosure

When your information is shared with a third-party service provider (Stripe or Netlify), it is transmitted exclusively through encrypted, secure channels:

  • Stripe: Booking details are sent via an encrypted HTTPS API call from our Netlify serverless function directly to Stripe's servers. No card data passes through CarefulTO's systems at any point in this process.
  • Netlify: Form data is submitted over HTTPS and processed within Netlify's secure infrastructure before being delivered to us.

There is no manual handoff of your data to any third party. We do not print, fax, or verbally relay your personal information to external parties. We do not share your information with data brokers, analytics companies, advertising networks, or social media platforms.

Your delivery addresses stay private. The recipient of your parcel is not provided with your personal contact details, and your details are not shared with the sender (if you are the recipient). We handle deliveries discreetly, as befitting a courier trusted with confidential documents.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

Booking Records

Completed booking records — including name, contact details, pickup and delivery addresses, parcel type, and booking date — are retained for a reasonable operational period for accounting, tax compliance, and dispute resolution purposes. Records that are no longer required are securely deleted.

Delivery Photos

Proof-of-delivery photographs are shared with you upon delivery and retained by CarefulTO for a short period sufficient to resolve any claim or dispute (aligned with the 48-hour claim window in our Terms of Service). Photos are then deleted from our records.

Payment Records

CarefulTO does not store payment card data. Stripe retains transaction records in accordance with their own data retention policies and applicable financial regulations. Your Stripe-issued receipt is the authoritative record of your payment.

Email Correspondence

Emails exchanged with CarefulTO — including booking confirmations and delivery updates — are retained in our email account for operational purposes. You may request deletion of your correspondence at any time (see Section 8).

7. Security Practices

CarefulTO takes the security of your personal information seriously and implements practices appropriate to the nature of our business and the sensitivity of the data we handle.

Encrypted Communications

Our entire website operates over HTTPS, ensuring that all data transmitted between your browser and our website — including your booking form submission — is encrypted in transit using TLS (Transport Layer Security).

Payment Security

Payment card data is handled exclusively by Stripe, which maintains PCI-DSS Level 1 certification — the highest standard for payment card security. CarefulTO's servers and systems never receive, process, or store your card number, expiry date, or CVV. This is by design. The moment you click "Proceed to Secure Payment," you are on Stripe's infrastructure, not ours.

Limited Data Access

CarefulTO is an owner-operated business. Your personal information is accessed only by the owner in the course of fulfilling your booking. There are no employees, contractors, or third parties with routine access to customer data.

No Third-Party Tracking

We do not embed third-party tracking scripts, advertising pixels, or analytics tools on our website that would expose your browsing behaviour to external companies. Our site is intentionally lean — it exists to serve you, not to profile you.

Responsible Handling of Sensitive Documents

Many of our clients entrust us with legal contracts, medical records, and confidential business correspondence. These items are handled personally and discreetly. We do not photograph, copy, scan, or read the contents of documents we are engaged to deliver. Confidentiality in transit is an absolute standard for us.

No system is perfectly impenetrable, and we do not make representations to that effect. However, the security measures we have put in place — HTTPS-only communications, Stripe-handled payments, minimal data collection, and owner-only access — are designed to significantly reduce risk and limit the impact of any potential incident.

CIRA Membership

CarefulTO is a proud member of the Canadian Internet Registration Authority (CIRA) — the national not-for-profit organization that manages the .ca domain and champions a trusted, secure Canadian internet. As a CIRA member, CarefulTO is part of a community of businesses and individuals committed to a safer online experience for all Canadians. This membership reflects our ongoing investment in operating with transparency, security, and accountability — the same values we bring to every delivery we make.

8. Your Rights

As a resident of Ontario or Canada, you have rights regarding your personal information under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation. These include:

  • Right to access — you may request a summary of the personal information CarefulTO holds about you
  • Right to correction — if information we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you may request that it be corrected
  • Right to deletion — you may request that we delete personal information we hold about you, subject to our obligations under applicable law (such as retaining records for tax or accounting purposes)
  • Right to withdraw consent — where your information is processed on the basis of consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time (note that withdrawal may affect our ability to provide or confirm delivery services)
  • Right to lodge a complaint — if you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us in writing at info@carefulto.ca. We will respond to all privacy requests within a reasonable time, typically within 10 business days.

We will never retaliate against, penalize, or discriminate against any person for exercising their privacy rights. Requesting access to or deletion of your data will have no effect on the quality of service you receive from CarefulTO.
